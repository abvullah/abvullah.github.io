okay, so we all are human beings who are naturally into doing many things.

but at the same time, we are scared to take the risk that will come alongside the thing that we are planning to do and well, that is the greatest fear that one can even deal with because of the fact, we as human beings are scared of hearing the scary word: no.

so, let's just try. because at the end of the day, it takes 99 nos only to get one yes.

and that's all you need.
